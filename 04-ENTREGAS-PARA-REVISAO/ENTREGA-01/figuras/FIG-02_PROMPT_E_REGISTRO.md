## Prompt final

> Create a new vertical conceptual editorial illustration for the nonfiction book “Fuga Identitária”, using the attached FIG-01 only as a strict visual-language reference, not as a composition to copy. Subject: “Duas fugas, o mesmo ciclo de alívio e terceirização.” Show a single anonymous human silhouette near the lower center facing an inner void or pressure point. From the discomfort at the center, two distinct looping paths emerge. The left path suggests cognitive escape: dispersed waves, noise, scrolling-like repetition, fragments, circular motion, temporary relief, then a return toward the unresolved center. The right path suggests identitary escape: sharper geometric layers, borrowed masks/shapes, rigid grouping, temporary certainty, then dependence looping back toward the same unresolved center. Both loops subtly converge below or behind the figure into one dark shared root, symbolizing outsourced authorship. Preserve a thin vertical vermilion axis through the figure as the possibility of recovered authorship, but make it interrupted or faint within the loops and clearer at the center. Style: sophisticated literary-social-science book illustration; tactile cut-paper collage, archival paper grain, restrained topographic contour lines, minimal abstract geometry, mature, sober, psychologically intelligent. Palette: ivory #F4EFE6, deep navy #0B1020, muted teal #5F7F83, charcoal #20242B, vermilion #D84A3A. High contrast suitable for a 14x21 cm printed page. Generous quiet negative space, clean focal hierarchy. No facial features, no identifiable person, no religious/political/LGBT symbols, no diagnostic imagery, no words, no letters, no captions, no watermark, no logo. Portrait 2:3 composition, print-ready, extremely refined.

## Leitura editorial

- o percurso orgânico e disperso representa a fuga cognitiva;
- o percurso geométrico, com máscaras e formas rígidas, representa a fuga identitária;
- ambos retornam ao desconforto não examinado;
- a raiz escura comum representa a terceirização da autoria;
- a linha vermelha vertical preserva a possibilidade de recuperar um eixo;
- a figura não contém informação indispensável que não esteja explicada no texto;
- não há pessoa identificável, símbolo de grupo, diagnóstico ou texto incorporado.
